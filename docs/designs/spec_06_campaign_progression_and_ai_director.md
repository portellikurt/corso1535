# Spec 06: Historical Timeline (1535–1565), Tech Unlocks & AI Director

## 1. Campaign Modes
1. **Historical Sandbox (`1535–1565`):** Starts in Spring 1535 with historical faction borders, dynamic macro-events triggered on historical dates (or accelerated by player actions), and open-ended victory paths (Trade Magnate, Grand Admiral, Sovereign Governor, or Renegade Corsair King).
2. **Free-Play Endless Sandbox:** Randomized resource gluts/famines, customizable starting ship (*Fusta*, *Caravel*, or *War Galley*), and dynamic faction wars without scripted historical railroading.

---

## 2. Historical Event Timeline & World Shifts
In Historical Sandbox mode, major geopolitical events reshape trade routes, mercenary contracts, and naval tech availability:

* **Summer 1535 — The Conquest of Tunis:** Emperor Charles V launches a massive allied armada from Sardinia and Sicily to retake Tunis and La Goleta from Hayreddin Barbarossa. Players aligned with Spain/Malta receive lucrative troop-convoy escort contracts; Barbary-aligned players earn triple bounties raiding Christian supply lines.
* **1538 — League of Preveza & Mediterranean Escalation:** Ottoman and Christian battle fleets expand dramatically, unlocking **Tier 2 Heavy War Galleys** and standardized bronze bow-chasers across all major shipyards.
* **1543 — Franco-Ottoman Wintering at Toulon:** Temporary diplomatic alignment shifts allow Renegade and Barbary corsairs to trade covertly in select northern ports, disrupting Habsburg grain routes.
* **1551 — The Siege of Tripoli & Dragut's Rise:** Barbary fleets under Turgut Reis (Dragut) launch a major offensive against Hospitaller Tripoli and Gozo—creating high-stakes defense missions for Maltese players or conquest opportunities for Barbary players.
* **1555–1565 — The Galleon & Great Siege Era:** Unlocks **Tier 3 Early Galleons and Heavy Galleasses**, culminating in the existential struggle for Malta's Grand Harbour.

## 3. Technology & Blueprint Progression
Technological advancement (`1535` Crossbows/Swivel Guns $\rightarrow$ `1565` Broadside Galleons) is driven by **three parallel vectors** so players are never arbitrarily time-gated if they invest heavily:

1. **Global Timeline Drift:** Base hull and artillery tiers gradually appear in major shipyards as campaign years advance.
2. **Shipyard Guild Investment:** Funding a port's **Arsenal & Foundry** accelerates local blueprint unlocks by up to $8\text{ years}$ ahead of the historical curve.
3. **Reverse Engineering via Capture:** Capturing an advanced enemy vessel (e.g., seizing a Venetian *Nave* or early Spanish *Galleon*) and towing it to your own shipyard allows master shipwrights to study the hull, permanently unlocking its blueprint for construction.

---

## 4. Strategic AI Fleet Director
To keep the Central Mediterranean alive without cheating fog-of-war, the AI Director manages four autonomous fleet archetypes:

* **Merchant Convoys (*Patrician* Agents):** Pathfind between ports using the exact same supply/demand price matrix as the player. They hire escort galleys when piracy spikes along a corridor.
* **State Patrol Squadrons:** Launch from Tier 2 ports to hunt ships with active bounties or unverified prizes, enforcing naval blockades during wartime.
* **AI Corsair Captains (*Carati* Peers):** Rival privateers operating out of Birgu, Algiers, Tunis, and Djerba who raid coastal villages, compete for prize targets, and sell shares in their expeditions on the port exchanges.
* **Treasure & Pilgrim Fleets:** Periodic high-value seasonal convoys (e.g., Spanish silver transports from Naples to Palermo, or Ottoman tribute galleys from Alexandria to Algiers) announced via port tavern rumors—creating focal points for multi-faction ambushes and escorts.
